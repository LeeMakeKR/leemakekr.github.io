# Seoul Maker Faire 2026

This year I took part in Seoul Maker Faire, held at DDP in Dongdaemun.

I brought the Poet Camera, the 1D Arcade, a mecanum wheel car, a 3D printed escapement, and a few other pieces.
As always, the builds that were hardest to make and packed with the most technology, the ones I most want to show off as the person who built them, drew less attention than simple, eye-catching pieces like the 1D Arcade.

This year I added lines like
'I make these as a hobby'
'They are not for sale'
to my personal introduction sign, because the same questions come up over and over.
Maybe it is because Koreans chase efficiency even in their free time.
There is a culture of assuming that time is only worth spending if it leads to a result or some kind of gain.
Perhaps that is why saying 'I make it as a hobby' seems to strike people as curious.

A gathering of people who simply enjoy making things is exactly what I think Maker Faire is.
