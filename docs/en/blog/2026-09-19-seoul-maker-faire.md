# Seoul Maker Faire 2026

This year I took part in Seoul Maker Faire, held at DDP in Dongdaemun.

I brought the Poetry Camera, the 1D Arcade, a mecanum wheel car, a 3D printed escapement, and a few other pieces.<br>
As always, the builds that were hardest to make and packed with the most technology, the ones I most want to show off as the person who built them, drew less attention than simple, eye-catching pieces like the 1D Arcade.<br><br>

This year I added lines like<br>
'I make these as a hobby'<br>
'They are not for sale'<br>
to my personal introduction sign, because the same questions come up over and over.<br>
Maybe it is because Koreans chase efficiency even in their free time.<br>
There is a culture of assuming that time is only worth spending if it leads to a result or some kind of gain.<br>
Perhaps that is why saying 'I make it as a hobby' seems to strike people as curious.<br><br>

A gathering of people who simply enjoy making things is exactly what I think Maker Faire is.<br><br>
